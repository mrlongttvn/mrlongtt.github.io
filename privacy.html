Privacy Policy for Gas vs EV

Effective Date: May 2026

Gas vs EV (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work with minimal data.

Information you enter
The App may store information you manually enter, such as gas price, vehicle MPG, electric rate, charging speed, battery size, EV range, and selected vehicle details for comparison.

Vehicle lookup data
If you use the vehicle lookup feature, the App sends your selected year, make, model, and trim requests to FuelEconomy.gov to retrieve public MPG information.

Information we do not collect
The App does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, or health data.

2. How We Use Information

The App uses information only to perform gas versus EV cost calculations, remember recent inputs on your device, retrieve vehicle MPG data, and support app functionality.

3. Data Storage

User-entered values are stored locally on your device. The developer does not currently maintain a server database for personal information through this App.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may connect to FuelEconomy.gov to retrieve public vehicle MPG data.

5. Data Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Tracking and Analytics

The App does not currently use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

The App is designed to minimize data collection. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may stop using the vehicle lookup feature at any time and clear locally stored app data by deleting the App from your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by a revised effective date.
